for deprecation / removal we should take things that are deprecated/dropped in instructure/ui* or instructure-design-tokens patches (Major.Minor.Patch) and mark them as `deprecated` but keep support until the next minor release.  So if 11.7.4 drops something, we mark it as 'deprected' until 11.8.0 drops, and then we cut a minor version of the consumer to.  So 0.1.3 -> 0.1.4 (deprecated) -> 0.2.0 (removed) for example.  If new deprecations are introduced in 11.8.0 (for example) then our 0.2.0 marks them as deprecated until 11.9.0 or 12.0.0.  This allows us to auto-document when a thing is deprecated, and indicate when it will be removed - by minor version.  Our minor versions can still move on their own so if we had already moved to 0.2.0 and then additional deprecations were added that would move us to 0.2.1 and then 11.8.0 would move us to 0.3.0, for example.
