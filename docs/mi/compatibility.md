# Hototahitanga

<!-- I hangaia e `vp run sync:compatibility`. Kaua e whakatika ā-ringa; whakahaerehia te kaituhi, kātahi ka commit. -->

Kei tēnei whārangi ngā pūtake Instructure UI ka whakatauria e tēnei hanga pantoken, me ngā kiritaki e whakamahi ana. He motuhake ngā putanga ūnga: ko ngā tukunga kua manatoko anake ka whakapuakina, ā, me arotake ngā tukunga hou ake i te tukunga manatoko whakamutunga. Hangaia anō mā `vp run sync:compatibility`; ka hapa te arowhai `gate:compatibility` mēnā ka rerekē.

## Ngā pūtake o runga

## Te arotake i ngā tukunga ūnga

Ka whakatuwheratia e te rerengamahi tukunga ūnga ā-wiki tētahi take arotake mō ngā tukunga WordPress pūmau hou me ngā taiao whai peer npm kua āta whakaritea. Mō ērā atu ūnga, ka tirohia e ngā kaitiaki ngā tuhipoka tukunga whaimana o te tūāpapa ia wiki, ā, ka whakatuwhera ā-ringa i tētahi take arotake; ki te kore he take, ehara tēnā i te tohu kua tautokona tētahi tukunga hou. Hei whakawhānui i tētahi whakapuakitanga:

1. Tirohia te hoahoa putanga o te tukunga, ngā panonitanga whakauru rānei, me te pūurutau kei tēnei ripanga.
2. Whakahaerehia te whakahau hototahitanga a te pūurutau me te tukunga kaitono, hei tauira `vp run @pantoken/postcss#check:compatibility -- 8.5.29`. Ka pānui te whakahau taunoa i ia tāurunga `testedVersions` mai i tēnei rēhita; ka whakaae ngā taiao takirua ki ngā haki hoa.
3. Mō ngā kaitono i kitea mā te whāngai, whakamahia `vp run downstream:test`; ka puritia ngā nganatanga i rahua ki `scripts/release/downstream-compatibility.json`. Whai muri i te arotake angitu, whakahaerehia `vp run downstream:bless --package @pantoken/name --version x.y.z` hei whakahou i te whakapuakitanga.
4. Whakahaerehia he arowhai taiao whakahaere tūturu, he arowhai whakahiato, he arowhai taputapu hanga rānei, i te tukunga mōkito me te tukunga hou. Mēnā e tika ana, whakahāngaitia anō te hōputu putanga o nāianei; kaua e tāpiri i ngā pūrongo hōputu tuku iho whakarara.
5. Whakahoutia `scripts/release/target-compatibility.json` ina oti pai ngā arowhai, kātahi ka whakahaerehia `vp run sync:compatibility`, hanga anō i ngā tuhinga API Ingarihi, ā, tāpirihia he changeset. Ka noho tarewa tonu ngā tukunga hou ake kia uru rā anō taua panonitanga kua arotakengia. Ka whakahaerehia motuhaketia ngā tuhinga kua whakamāoritia.

## Ngā mea kua whakakorea

He tohu i whakatakahia e te pūtake o runga, engari kei te tuku tonu a pantoken hei shim hototahi. Ka mahi tonu ia tae noa ki te whakamahinga o tana putanga iti `Removed in` o runga, ā, i taua wā ka whakamutua te shim, ka tukuna he putanga iti hou mō te kiritaki. Ka tuku whakamua tētahi shim ki tētahi tohu `Replacement`, ka whakatio rānei i tōna uara mōhiotia whakamutunga.
