# Hototahitanga

<!-- I hangaia e `vp run sync:compatibility`. Kaua e whakatika ā-ringa; whakahaerehia te pūtanga, ka tuku. -->

Ka tuhia ki tēnei whārangi ngā pūtake Instructure UI e whakatauria ana e tēnei hanga pantoken, me ngā kaiwhakamahi o aua pūtake. He motuhake ngā putanga whāinga: ka whakapuakina ngā tukunga kua manatoko anake, ā, me arotake ngā tukunga i muri mai i te tukunga manatoko whakamutunga. Hangaia anō mā `vp run sync:compatibility`; ka rahua te arowhai `gate:compatibility` mēnā ka rerekē.

## Te arotake i ngā tukunga whāinga

Ka whakatuwheratia e te rerengamahi tukunga whāinga ā-wiki tētahi take arotake mō ngā tukunga WordPress pūmau hou me ngā taiao e whai hoa npm mārama ana. Mō ērā atu whāinga, ka tirohia e ngā kaitiaki ngā pārongo tukunga mana o te tūāpapa ia wiki, ā, ka whakatuwhera ā-ringa i tētahi take arotake; ehara te kore take i te tohu e tautokona ana tētahi tukunga hou. Hei whakawhānui i tētahi whakapuakitanga:

1. Tirohia te hoahoa putanga o te tukunga, ngā panonitanga whakaurunga rānei, me te whāurutau kei tēnei ripanga.
2. Whakahaerehia te whakahau hototahitanga a te whāurutau me te tukunga kaitono, hei tauira
   `vp run @pantoken/postcss#check:compatibility -- 8.5.29`. Ka pānui te whakahau taunoa i ia tāurunga
   `testedVersions` mai i tēnei rēhita; ka whakaae ngā taiao takirua ki ō rātou kara hoa.
3. Mō ngā kaitono i kitea mā te whāngai, whakamahia `vp run downstream:test`; ka puritia ngā nganatanga i rahua ki
   `scripts/release/downstream-compatibility.json`. Whai muri i te arotake angitu, whakahaerehia
   `vp run downstream:bless --package @pantoken/name --version x.y.z` hei whakahou i te whakapuakitanga.
4. Whakahaerehia he arowhai taiao manaaki, kaiwhakahiato, taputapu hanga rānei ki te tukunga iti rawa me te tukunga hou. Mēnā e tika ana, whakahāngaitia anō te hōputu putanga o nāianei; kaua e tāpiri kaiwhakaputa hōputu tawhito whakarara.
5. Whakahoutia `scripts/release/target-compatibility.json` i muri anake i te paahitanga o ngā arowhai, kātahi ka whakahaerehia
   `vp run sync:compatibility`, hanga anō ngā tuhinga API Ingarihi, ka tāpiri i tētahi changeset. Ka noho tārewa tonu ngā tukunga hou ake kia tau rā anō taua panonitanga kua arotakengia. Ka whakahaerehia motuhaketia ngā tuhinga kua whakamāoritia.

## Ngā whakakorenga

Ko ngā tāpae i whakakorea ake e te pūtake, engari kei te tuku tonu a pantoken hei paparanga hototahitanga. Ka mau tonu ia kia tangohia rā anō tōna tukunga iti pūtake `Removed in`; i taua wā ka whakamutua te paparanga, ka tukuna he tukunga iti hou mō te kaiwhakamahi. Ka tuku whakamua tētahi paparanga ki tētahi tāpae `Replacement`, ka mau rānei ki tōna uara mōhiotia whakamutunga.
