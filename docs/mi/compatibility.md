# Hototahitanga

<!-- I hangaia tēnei whārangi e `vp run sync:compatibility`. Kaua e whakatika ā-ringa; whakahaerehia te kaituhi, kātahi ka commit. -->

Kei tēnei whārangi ngā pūtake Instructure UI e whakatauria ana e tēnei hanga pantoken, me ngā kiritaki e whakamahi ana. He motuhake ngā putanga ūnga: ka kīia ko ngā putanga kua manatoko anake, ā, me arotake ngā putanga i muri mai i te putanga manatoko whakamutunga. Hangaia anō mā te `vp run sync:compatibility`; ka rahua te taki `gate:compatibility` mēnā kua rerekē.

## Te arotake i ngā putanga ūnga

Ka whakatuwheratia e te rerengamahi putanga ūnga ia wiki tētahi take arotake mō ngā putanga WordPress pūmau hou me ngā taiao rauropi e whai npm peer mārama ana. Mō ērā atu ūnga, ka tirohia e ngā kaitiaki ngā tuhipoka tuku whaimana o te tūāpapa ia wiki, ā, ka whakatuwhera ā-ringa i tētahi take arotake; ki te kore he take, ehara tēnā i te tohu kua tautokona tētahi putanga hou. Hei whakawhānui i tētahi kī taurangi:

1. Tirohia te aronuinga putanga, ngā panonitanga whakauru rānei, me te whāurutau kei tēnei ripanga.
2. Whakahaerehia te whakahau hototahitanga a te whāurutau me te putanga kaitono, hei tauira
   `vp run @pantoken/postcss#check:compatibility -- 8.5.29`. Ka pānui te whakahau taunoa i ia tāurunga
   `testedVersions` mai i tēnei rēhita; ka whakaaetia ngā kara hoa mō ngā taiao takirua.
3. Mō ngā kaitono i kitea mā te whāngai, whakamahia te `vp run downstream:test`; ka puritia ngā whakamātau
   i rahua ki `scripts/release/downstream-compatibility.json`. Whai muri i te arotake angitu, whakahaerehia
   `vp run downstream:bless --package @pantoken/name --version x.y.z` hei whakahou i te kī taurangi.
4. Whakahaerehia he taki tūturu mā te taiao manaaki, te kaiwhakahiato, te taputapu hanga rānei, i te putanga iti rawa me te putanga hou. Whakaritea anō te hōputu putanga o nāianei mēnā e tika ana; kaua e tāpiri i ngā kaiwhakaputa hōputu tuku iho whakarara.
5. Whakahoutia `scripts/release/target-compatibility.json` i muri noa iho i te paahitanga o ngā taki, kātahi ka whakahaere
   `vp run sync:compatibility`, hanga anō ngā tuhinga API Ingarihi, ā, tāpirihia he changeset. Ka noho tārewa tonu ngā putanga hou ake kia uru rā anō taua panonitanga kua arotakengia. Ka whakahaerehia motuhaketia ngā tuhinga kua whakamāoritia.

## Ngā whakakorenga

Ko ngā token i tangohia ake e te upstream, engari e tukuna tonutia ana e pantoken hei shim hototahitanga. Ka mau tonu te mahi a ia mea kia tangohia rā anō tana putanga iti upstream `Removed in`; i taua wā ka whakamutua te shim, ā, ka tukuna he putanga iti mō te kiritaki. Ka tuku whakamua tētahi shim ki tētahi token `Replacement`, ka whakatio rānei i tōna uara mōhiotia whakamutunga.
