# TinyMCE Oxide source

The `src/less` and `src/svg` directories in this folder are copied from TinyMCE `8.9.1`, commit
`f9dc8185c2d2f7c7d3759ecb569931cee7d530bb`, from `modules/oxide`.

Oxide is licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later by Tiny Technologies, Inc. The copied source remains under
that license. The theme entrypoints under `src/less/skins/ui` and `src/less/skins/content` contain
the project-specific overrides and are compiled into the published CSS assets.
